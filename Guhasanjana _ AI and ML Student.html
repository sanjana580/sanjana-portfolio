Projects

Intelligent subtitle-to-speech conversion with real-time audio synchronization

Problem
Subtitles only help people who can read them. This system turns them into speech that matches the video's timing.
What I built
An AI-powered pipeline in Python. It extracts and processes subtitle files with FFmpeg and pysrt, generates speech with Google Text-to-Speech (gTTS), syncs the audio to subtitle timestamps with pydub, and merges it into the video with MoviePy.
Result
Automated subtitle narration that makes videos more accessible.
